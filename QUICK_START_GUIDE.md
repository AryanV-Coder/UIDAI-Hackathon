## 🔑 Key Findings (At a Glance)

### Finding 1: Geographic Disparities
- **Gap Ratio:** Top district **50-100x** higher than bottom
- **Bottom 15%:** 145 districts with <500 enrollments/month average
- **Solution:** Targeted intervention (₹36.25 cr)

### Finding 2: Age Composition Skew
- **Current:** 65% children, 31% youth, <4% adults
- **Target:** 35:30:35 (balanced)
- **Solution:** Adult enrollment campaigns (₹100 cr)

### Finding 3: Growth Momentum
- **Strong Growth:** 305 districts (33.6%) with positive slope
- **Strong Decline:** 517 districts (57.0%) with negative slope
- **Solution:** Knowledge transfer from leaders (₹75L)

### Finding 4: Service Clustering
- **4 Clusters Identified** via K-Means
- **Enables:** Differentiated policy design by cluster/tier
- **Solution:** Cluster-specific interventions

### Finding 5: Anomaly Events
- **Spikes (Positive):** 236 events (4.7%) → Replication opportunities
- **Drops (Negative):** 62 events (1.2%) → Investigation priorities
- **Solution:** Anomaly investigation protocol (₹40 cr)

### Finding 6: Operational Volatility
- **High-Volatility:** 241 districts (top 25%)
- **Root Cause:** Campaign-driven model (unsustainable)
- **Solution:** Transition to steady-flow (₹75 cr)
